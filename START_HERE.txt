HEALTHASSIST AGENTIC PLATFORM v2

1. Extract the ZIP; open this whole folder in VS Code.
2. Double-click run_windows.bat or follow README.md (Python 3.12).
3. Use Overview -> Load fictional training records to explore without an API key.
4. Select an encounter, then use Clinical Workspace, Care Tasks and Reports.
5. Open AI Care Agent, supply your provider key, and opt into context sharing.
6. Upload ALL files to GitHub; choose root app.py in Streamlit Cloud.

Read FEATURE_GUIDE.md for all modules and tested workflows.
Research support only: not diagnosis, prescribing or an emergency service.
